# Protocol badge icons

One round 16 px badge per protocol in `hicolor/scalable/apps/curlew-protocol-<backend>.svg`: a flat
disc in a colour near the protocol's own, tinted to the GNOME palette, with a simple white glyph
(Matrix: dark disc, green glyph and rim). They read on light and dark backgrounds.

| `curlew-protocol-telegram` | `#1c71d8` | paper plane | drawn for Curlew | same as Curlew |
| `curlew-protocol-signal` | `#3a76f0` | dashed ring, speech bubble | drawn for Curlew | same as Curlew |
| `curlew-protocol-whatsapp` | `#26a269` | bubble with handset | drawn for Curlew | same as Curlew |
| `curlew-protocol-matrix` | `#241f31` | `[m]` in green | drawn for Curlew | same as Curlew |
| `curlew-protocol-xmpp` | `#e66100` | crossed strokes | drawn for Curlew | same as Curlew |

No third-party artwork is copied, so no external licence or attribution applies (CC0 sets such as
simple-icons were considered; the glyphs were drawn fresh to share one frame). Delta Chat is mail
with an opt-in marker (ADR 0014), so it uses the mail badge.

**Trademarks.** The shapes and colours are inspired by, not copies of, the protocols' marks. They
are not official and imply no endorsement by or affiliation with WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Matrix,
XMPP or Delta Chat. Colour never carries the meaning alone: every badge has a label or tooltip.
